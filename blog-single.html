Preparing for your first divorce consultation can help you make the most of your meeting with an attorney. A well-prepared divorce consultation gives you the information and confidence needed to make informed decisions about your future. By organizing your documents, identifying your priorities, and openly discussing your concerns, you and your attorney can work together to develop a legal strategy that protects your rights and supports your long-term goals.

Many people attend their first consultation without the necessary documents or a clear understanding of what they want to achieve. This often results in longer consultations, unanswered questions, and delays in developing an effective legal strategy.

Before your consultation, gather important documents such as your marriage certificate, identification, financial statements, tax returns, bank account information, property records, insurance policies, and any existing legal agreements. If children are involved, bring information related to custody, education, healthcare, and daily living arrangements.
